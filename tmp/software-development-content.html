Before we begin

A few useful
answers.

Can you work with our existing software?

We start by reviewing the current system, available access and documentation. That helps us assess whether extending, integrating or rebuilding it is the right approach.

What do you need to estimate a project?

Your business goals, intended users, main workflows, existing systems and any timing constraints are a useful starting point. We can help clarify requirements when the scope is still taking shape.

How long will development take?

The timeline depends on the scope, integrations, complexity and review process. We agree on a delivery plan after discussing the requirements.

How are changes to scope handled?

We discuss the requested change, its effect on the delivery plan and the work involved, then agree on the next steps before proceeding.

What about source code, handover and support?

Ownership, source access, documentation, handover and ongoing support are agreed in the project scope and contract. We discuss these requirements before work starts.
